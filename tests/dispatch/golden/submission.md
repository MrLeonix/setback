# Objection to Development Application

**To:** Georges River Council
**Development Application:** PAN-661190
**Property:** 65A Vista Street, Sans Souci NSW 2219
**Exhibition period:** 20 August 2026 to 3 September 2026

I am writing to formally object to the above Development Application, exhibited for public comment during the period stated above. I ask that the following grounds be taken into account under section 4.15 of the *Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979* (NSW) in determining this application.

## Grounds of objection

### 1. Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (NSW) s4.15(1)(a)

The proposed roof ridge sits at 10.2m, exceeding the 9m maximum height of buildings control that applies to this site under the Georges River LEP 2021.

*Reference: Statement of Environmental Effects, page 14.*

### 2. Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979 (NSW) s4.15(1)(b)

The shadow diagrams show the rear yard of 63 Vista Street in total shadow from 2pm to 4pm at the winter solstice, well beyond the amenity loss the DCP considers acceptable.

*Reference: Shadow Diagrams, page 3.*
*Annotated evidence: overshadowing-3pm-annotated.png.*
